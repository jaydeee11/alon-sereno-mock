Welcome

A shoreline
made for stillness

Alon Sereno is a small resort built around one idea: that the best luxury is time. Handwoven rattan, native hardwoods and open-air pavilions frame the bay, and every villa faces the water.

Mornings begin with the sound of the tide and a slow breakfast on your deck. Afternoons belong to the lagoon, the spa, or nothing at all. Our team of just forty welcomes each guest by name.
